Hardened Mode

In standard mode, Dry Toast is a gentle gatekeeper on your home screen. When you want Android itself to enforce your boundaries, optional Hardened Mode uses Android’s built-in Device Owner capability instead of invasive Accessibility Service screen-scraping.

OS-Level App Suspension

Blocked apps and resting Guarded apps are suspended directly by the Android OS. They cannot be opened from Recent Apps, notifications, deep links, or Play Store shortcuts until your rules allow them. Your default Phone dialer stays permanently exempt.

Guarded Session Leases

When you complete a friction checkpoint for a Guarded app in Hardened Mode, Dry Toast temporarily unsuspends it for a configurable grace period (0–30 minutes) and automatically re-suspends it once the session ends.

Tamper Resistance

Locks Dry Toast as your persistent default home screen and prevents impulse force-stopping, storage clearing, or uninstalling Dry Toast while Hardened Mode is active.

Setup & Removal

Hardened Mode can be enabled on an existing phone without a factory reset using our browser-based USB setup in the Console (temporarily sign out of accounts, activate over USB, then sign right back in), or provisioned on a fresh phone via a 6-Tap QR code. You can deactivate it cleanly anytime from the Console without wiping your device.
