Features

Everything you need to use your phone deliberately, and nothing designed to keep you scrolling.

Typographic Home Screen

No app icons, no notification dots, no news feeds. Your home screen shows only the pinned tools you choose in plain text, paired with subtle quick actions and immediate access to your phone dialer.

Zero-Latency Fuzzy Search

Open search from the home screen with your keyboard ready. Type a couple of letters and Threshold filters instantly, automatically launching when only one app matches.

Intentional Friction Checkpoints

Instead of hard-blocking every app, mark distracting apps as Guarded. Opening a Guarded app asks for a short deliberate action first: holding a deliberate pause, sorting a quick sequence, or typing your intention.

Offline by Design

Threshold does not declare android.permission.INTERNET. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics, and no ads. Your rules and activity never leave your phone.
