Hardened Mode (Device Owner)

Provision OS-level Device Owner protection on a fresh install, or deactivate Hardened Mode on an enrolled phone.

Fresh Install Setup

Recommended

6-Tap Welcome Screen QR Setup

Hardened Mode should be enabled on a brand-new or freshly factory-reset phone before signing into Google accounts.

  1. Turn on a brand-new or freshly factory-reset Android phone.
  2. On Android’s very first Welcome screen, tap the screen 6 times rapidly in the same spot to open Android’s built-in QR scanner.
  3. Connect to Wi-Fi when prompted, then scan the QR code below.
  4. Android will download the signed Threshold APK, verify its SHA-256 checksum, and set Threshold as Device Owner automatically.
6-Tap Welcome Screen Setup QR

Once Hardened Mode is active, also enable Settings Lock (/console) or Require Reboot for Settings Changes inside Threshold Settings so rules cannot be weakened from within the app.

USB / ADB command alternative (fresh device with zero accounts) 
# If Threshold is already installed on a fresh device with no accounts added:
adb shell dpm set-device-owner com.garan.threshold/.dpc.ThresholdDeviceAdminReceiver

Step 01

Scan with your phone

Open your phone’s Camera or QR scanner and scan this code to open the hidden Disable Hardened Mode screen in Threshold.

threshold://hardened/disable

Step 02

Enter the 3-digit challenge

Your phone displays a 3-digit challenge code. Type it below to get your 6-digit verification code, then enter that 6-digit code on your phone to unsuspend all apps and release Device Owner protection.

6-Digit Code
· · · · · ·

Keep Threshold open on your phone while entering the code. Leaving the screen on your phone generates a new 3-digit challenge.

USB / ADB command alternative 
# Deactivate Hardened Mode via ADB
adb shell am broadcast -a com.garan.threshold.action.DISABLE_HARDENED_MODE -n com.garan.threshold/.settings.SettingsLockReceiver
