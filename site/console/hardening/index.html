Hardened Mode (Device Owner)

Provision OS-level Device Owner protection on a fresh install, or deactivate Hardened Mode on an enrolled phone.

Viewing on your phone? Open drytoast.app/hardened-mode on a computer or second screen so you can scan the QR code.

Step 01 · Pre-Reset Checklist

Back up your phone before resetting

If you are setting up Hardened Mode on an existing phone, you will factory reset it first. Take two minutes to confirm these backups before wiping:

What comes back automatically: After setup finishes and you sign into Google Play, your Contacts, Calendar, Gmail, Photos, Chrome, Play Store app library, and app-native cloud backups (WhatsApp, 2FA, password manager) sync right back.

What to expect in Device Owner mode: Android displays a small "Managed by Dry Toast" notice in Quick Settings and does not allow Work Profiles (such as Microsoft Intune), Private Space, or Samsung Secure Folder on the same device. You can deactivate Hardened Mode cleanly anytime from the Disable tab without wiping your phone.

Step 02 · Fresh Install Setup

6-Tap QR

Scan on Android's Welcome screen

Hardened Mode is provisioned on a brand-new or freshly factory-reset phone before signing into Google accounts.

  1. Turn on a brand-new or freshly factory-reset Android phone.
  2. On Android's very first Welcome screen, tap the screen 6 times rapidly in the same spot to open Android's built-in QR scanner.
  3. Connect to Wi-Fi when prompted, then scan the QR code below.
  4. Android will download the signed Dry Toast APK, verify its SHA-256 checksum, and set Dry Toast as Device Owner automatically.
6-Tap Welcome Screen Setup QR
USB / ADB command alternative (fresh device with zero accounts) 
# If Dry Toast is already installed on a fresh device with no accounts added:
adb shell dpm set-device-owner com.garan.drytoast/.dpc.DryToastDeviceAdminReceiver

Step 01

Scan with your phone

Open your phone’s Camera or QR scanner and scan this code to open the hidden Disable Hardened Mode screen in Dry Toast.

drytoast://hardened/disable

Step 02

Enter the 3-digit challenge

Your phone displays a 3-digit challenge code. Type it below to get your 6-digit verification code, then enter that 6-digit code on your phone to unsuspend all apps and release Device Owner protection.
