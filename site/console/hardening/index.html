Hardened Mode (Device Owner)

Enable OS-level Device Owner protection on an existing phone without a factory reset, provision a fresh device via 6-Tap QR, or deactivate cleanly anytime.

Viewing on your phone? Open drytoast.app/console/hardening on a computer (Chrome, Edge, or Brave for USB setup, or any screen for the 6-Tap QR code).
Direct USB setup requires Chrome, Edge, or Brave on a computer. Your current browser does not expose WebUSB. Open this page in Chrome/Edge on a computer, or use the terminal ADB commands below.

Step 01 · Credentials Safety Check

Required

Confirm you can sign back into your accounts

Android only allows Device Owner setup when zero accounts are signed in. You will temporarily remove your accounts, enable Hardened Mode, and sign right back in. No apps, photos, messages, or local files are wiped.

Important: Before removing any account, be 100% certain you know your Google password (and any work, email, or messaging logins) and have your 2FA authenticator, passkey, or backup codes ready on a second device or written down.

100% local in your browser: USB commands run directly from this browser tab over your cable to your phone. Nothing is uploaded or sent to any server.

What to expect in Device Owner mode: Android shows a small "Managed by Dry Toast" notice in Quick Settings and does not allow Work Profiles (such as Microsoft Intune), Private Space, or Samsung Secure Folder on the same device. You can deactivate anytime from the Disable tab without wiping your phone.

Step 02 · USB Debugging

Not connected

Connect your phone via USB

  1. Make sure Dry Toast is installed on your phone (Google Play or GitHub Release).
  2. On your phone, open Settings → About phone and tap Build number 7 times to enable Developer options.
  3. Open Settings → System → Developer options, turn on USB debugging, and plug your phone into this computer with a USB data cable.

Step 04 · Activate & Confirm

Locked

Enable Hardened Mode

This button unlocks automatically as soon as all three checks above are green.

Hardened Mode is active on your phone.

Dry Toast now holds Android Device Owner privileges. Complete these final steps:

  1. Open Dry Toast → Settings → Hardened Mode and confirm STATUS: HARDENED MODE ACTIVE.
  2. Sign back into your Google account and any other accounts you removed. Your apps, photos, and local data are still intact.
  3. Turn USB debugging back off in Developer options, then unplug your USB cable.
Using Firefox/Safari or prefer terminal ADB? 
# 1. Confirm your phone is connected and authorized
adb devices

# 2. Open Passwords & accounts on your phone and remove all accounts
adb shell am start -a android.settings.SYNC_SETTINGS

# 3. Verify 0 accounts and only 1 user remain
adb shell dumpsys account | grep "Accounts:" && adb shell pm list users

# 4. Grant Device Owner to Dry Toast (then sign back into your accounts)
adb shell dpm set-device-owner com.garan.drytoast/.dpc.DryToastDeviceAdminReceiver

Step 02 · Fresh Install Setup

6-Tap QR

Scan on Android's Welcome screen

Hardened Mode is provisioned on a brand-new or freshly factory-reset phone before signing into Google accounts.

  1. Turn on a brand-new or freshly factory-reset Android phone.
  2. On Android's very first Welcome screen, tap the screen 6 times rapidly in the same spot to open Android's built-in QR scanner.
  3. Connect to Wi-Fi when prompted, then scan the QR code below.
  4. Android will download the signed Dry Toast APK, verify its SHA-256 checksum, and set Dry Toast as Device Owner automatically.
6-Tap Welcome Screen Setup QR

Step 01

Scan with your phone

Open your phone’s Camera or QR scanner and scan this code to open the hidden Disable Hardened Mode screen in Dry Toast.

drytoast://hardened/disable

Step 02

Enter the 3-digit challenge

Your phone displays a 3-digit challenge code. Type it below to get your 6-digit verification code, then enter that 6-digit code on your phone to unsuspend all apps and release Device Owner protection.
