Settings Lock & Unlock

Lock Dry Toast Settings to prevent impulse changes, or unlock them when you need to edit your rules.

Viewing on your phone? Open drytoast.app/console on a computer or second screen so you can scan the QR code below with this phone.

Step 01

Scan with your phone

Open your phone’s Camera or QR scanner on a separate screen and scan this code to open the Settings Lock screen in Dry Toast.

Step 02

Enter the 3-digit challenge

Your phone displays a 3-digit challenge code. Type it below to get your 6-digit verification code, then enter that 6-digit code on your phone.

6-Digit Code
· · · · · ·

Keep Dry Toast open on your phone while entering the code. Leaving the screen on your phone generates a new 3-digit challenge.

USB / ADB command alternative 
# Lock Dry Toast Settings
adb shell am broadcast -a com.garan.drytoast.action.LOCK_SETTINGS -n com.garan.drytoast/.settings.SettingsLockReceiver

# Unlock Dry Toast Settings
adb shell am broadcast -a com.garan.drytoast.action.UNLOCK_SETTINGS -n com.garan.drytoast/.settings.SettingsLockReceiver
