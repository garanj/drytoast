Use your phone on purpose.

Threshold is a quiet, text-only Android launcher. It strips away icons, badges, and feeds—leaving only the tools you chose to put in front of you.

Add brief friction to distracting apps, switch rules automatically by time, location, or NFC tag, and lock everything down when you want zero escape hatches. Completely offline, with zero internet permission.
