Configuration

Press and hold on the home screen for three seconds (or type :settings in search) to configure your rules, profiles, and triggers.

App Rules (Allow, Guard, Block)

Every installed app sits in one of three states: Allowed (opens normally), Guarded (requires a friction checkpoint first), or Blocked (hidden from your home screen and search). Your default Phone dialer is always allowed.

Profiles & Cascading Rules

Set your baseline rules once, then create focused profiles like Work, Evening, or Weekend that override only the apps you want to tighten or relax for that context.

Schedules, Location & NFC Triggers

Switch profiles automatically on a time schedule, at local sunrise or sunset, when arriving at a place, or by tapping a physical NFC card on your desk or nightstand.

Settings Lock & Reboot-to-Apply

To prevent weakening your rules on impulse, turn on Require Reboot for Settings Changes so edits only take effect after a restart—or lock Threshold Settings completely using the Settings Lock Console.
