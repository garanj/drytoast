Privacy Policy

Application: Threshold Launcher (com.garan.threshold)
Last updated: November 19, 2026

Summary

Threshold Launcher does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.

Threshold Launcher does not request or possess the Android internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET). It is technically impossible for the application to transmit information off your device.

1. Information We Collect

None. We do not collect any personal information, device identifiers, location history, installed application lists, or usage telemetry. There are no user accounts, cloud servers, advertising SDKs, or analytics frameworks inside Threshold Launcher.

2. Local-Only Data Storage

All configuration data you create in Threshold Launcher (such as your pinned apps, focus profiles, schedules, local counters, and preferences) is stored exclusively on your device inside Android’s private application storage (with android:allowBackup="false"). Uninstalling the application permanently deletes all locally stored preferences.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used On-Device

Threshold Launcher uses the following Android permissions strictly locally on your device to function as a home screen replacement:

  • Installed Applications (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Required so the home screen and search drawer can list and launch the applications installed on your device. Your list of installed apps never leaves your device.
  • Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, Optional): Requested only if you explicitly create a location-based profile rule. Coordinates are evaluated locally on your device and are never transmitted or logged.
  • NFC (NFC, Optional): Used only if you pair a physical NFC tag to toggle a focus profile. Only the tag payload is matched locally on your device.
  • Alarms & Boot Scheduling (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used locally to trigger scheduled focus profile transitions on time and restore active schedules after a device restart.
  • Wallpaper & Haptics (SET_WALLPAPER, VIBRATE): Used locally to apply optional focus wallpapers and tactile feedback.
  • Device Administrator / Device Owner (BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN, Optional): Used only if you explicitly provision Hardened Mode to enforce OS-level app suspension for blocked and guarded apps during focus sessions.

4. Third-Party Services

Threshold Launcher integrates zero third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud SDKs.
